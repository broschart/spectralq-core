Mobile Network Infrastructure

Captures mobile cell towers (GSM, UMTS, LTE, 5G NR) in the defined area and displays them on the map with signal range and technology type.

How to use: Draw a zone on the map and click "Live". All known cell towers within the zone are shown. Compare snapshots over time to detect new towers, removed infrastructure or coverage gaps.

Forensic relevance: Changes in cell tower activity can indicate population movements, targeted network shutdowns, or the deployment of mobile surveillance equipment (IMSI catchers). Sudden disappearance of towers may signal infrastructure damage in conflict zones.

Data source: OpenCelliD — requires a free API key.
