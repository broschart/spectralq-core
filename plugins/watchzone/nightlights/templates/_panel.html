Nighttime Lights

Analyses nocturnal light emissions from NASA satellite imagery to detect changes in human activity on the ground.

How to use: Draw a zone on the map and click "Live". The system fetches VIIRS nighttime images and measures light intensity over time. Results are shown on the map and as a downloadable image. The time series is automatically integrated into the analysis timeline.

Forensic relevance: A sudden decrease in nighttime light can indicate power outages, population displacement or infrastructure destruction. Increases may signal new construction, military camps or industrial activity. Comparing light patterns before and after a conflict event provides strong visual evidence of impact.

Data source: NASA VIIRS Day/Night Band via GIBS — no API key required.
