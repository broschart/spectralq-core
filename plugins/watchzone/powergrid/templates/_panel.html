Power Grid & Load Data

Monitors power grid load across European countries in near real time.

How to use: Draw a zone covering one or more countries and click "Live". A 48-hour chart shows load progression with current, average, minimum and maximum values. The system highlights deviations from normal patterns.

Forensic relevance: Sudden load drops indicate blackouts, infrastructure damage or targeted shutdowns. Gradual declines may reflect economic disruption. Cross-referencing with conflict data or weather events helps distinguish intentional from natural causes.

Data source: ENTSO-E Transparency Platform — requires a free API token.
Draw observation areas on the map. ENTSO-E load data will be fetched automatically for the respective country.
