Air Quality & Particulates

Monitors air quality from ground-level sensor stations worldwide, covering particulate matter (PM2.5, PM10), ozone (O₃), nitrogen dioxide (NO₂), sulphur dioxide (SO₂) and carbon monoxide (CO).

How to use: Draw a zone on the map and click "Live". All monitoring stations in the area are listed with their latest readings and an air quality rating.

Forensic relevance: Sudden spikes in PM2.5 or SO₂ can indicate fires, explosions, industrial accidents or military activity. Persistent elevated NO₂ may point to increased heavy vehicle traffic (e.g. military logistics). Cross-referencing with satellite imagery and wind data helps trace pollution sources.

Data source: OpenAQ (global sensor network) — requires a free API key.
