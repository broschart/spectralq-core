NDVI / Vegetation

Measures vegetation density via satellite using the NDVI index (Normalized Difference Vegetation Index), which ranges from -1 (no vegetation) to +1 (dense, healthy vegetation).

How to use: Draw a zone on the map and click "Live". The system calculates the NDVI trend over the last 90 days, showing current, maximum, minimum and average values. The time series is automatically integrated into the analysis timeline under "Additional Sources".

Forensic relevance: Sudden NDVI decreases can indicate deforestation, bombing damage, wildfire aftermath or agricultural collapse. Gradual declines may point to drought or desertification. Comparing NDVI with conflict data helps assess environmental impact of military operations.

Data source: Copernicus Sentinel-2 — requires Copernicus credentials.
