Bluesky Keyword Monitor

Monitors keyword mentions on the Bluesky social network, tracking post volume and discourse patterns over time.

How to use: Create keyword groups with search terms and click "Live". The system shows post volume over time with daily averages and peak detection. Trend lines reveal whether a topic is gaining or losing traction.

Forensic relevance: As an increasingly popular alternative to X/Twitter, Bluesky is becoming relevant for tracking public discourse, especially among journalists, activists and policy communities. Detecting discourse shifts, viral narratives and coordinated posting patterns helps assess public resonance and identify potential influence campaigns.

Data source: Bluesky / AT Protocol — requires Bluesky credentials (handle + app password).
Enter keywords and create a zone. Posts on Bluesky will be counted automatically.
