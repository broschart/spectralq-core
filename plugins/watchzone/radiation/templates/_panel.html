Radiation Monitoring

Monitors ambient gamma dose rate from fixed measurement stations in the defined area.

How to use: Draw a zone on the map and click "Live". All stations in the area are displayed with their current readings. For German stations (BfS), a time-series chart shows the dose rate over time. You can toggle between validated and unvalidated data.

Anomaly detection: Elevated values above 0.3 µSv/h are automatically flagged. Normal background radiation in Central Europe is 0.05–0.18 µSv/h.

Coverage: BfS ODL provides data for ~1,600 stations in Germany. EURDEP covers ~37,000 stations across Europe.

Data sources: BfS ODL (Germany) + EURDEP (Europe) — no API key required.
Draw observation zones on the map. Gamma dose rate data will be fetched automatically for all stations in the zone.
