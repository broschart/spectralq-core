Seismic Activity

Records earthquakes within the defined area with magnitude, depth, location and time.

How to use: Draw a zone on the map and click "Live". All seismic events from the last 30 days are listed. Each event shows magnitude, depth, exact coordinates and a link to the USGS detail page. Community reports (DYFI — Did You Feel It?) and waveform data are available where reported.

Forensic relevance: Seismic data can help distinguish natural earthquakes from underground explosions (e.g. nuclear tests or large-scale demolitions). Shallow events with unusual waveform signatures are automatically highlighted. Useful for crisis regions and areas near nuclear facilities.

Data source: USGS Earthquake Catalog — no API key required.
Draw observation areas on the map. Earthquake activity (USGS) will be displayed automatically in the analysis timeline (under "Other Sources").
