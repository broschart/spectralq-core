Certificate Transparency

Monitors Certificate Transparency (CT) logs for specified domains, tracking all issued TLS/SSL certificates and newly discovered subdomains.

How to use: Add a domain and click "Live". The system queries crt.sh and lists all certificates with issuer, validity dates and associated subdomains. New subdomains discovered within the last 30 days are highlighted.

Forensic relevance: New certificates and subdomains are early indicators of campaign preparation — phishing sites, new infrastructure or service deployments. Monitoring a target domain's certificate history reveals hosting changes, CDN migrations and shadow IT. Wildcard certificates may indicate rapid infrastructure scaling.

Data source: crt.sh (Certificate Transparency Logs) — no API key required.
Add domains whose certificate transparency should be monitored. New subdomains and certificates are automatically detected.
