Traffic Conditions

Monitors traffic incidents, congestion, accidents and road closures in the defined area in real time.

How to use: Draw a zone on the map and click "Live". All current incidents are listed with severity level, type and location. The Flow Index shows overall traffic density compared to normal conditions.

Forensic relevance: Anomalies in traffic density can indicate major events, demonstrations, military convoys, evacuations or security cordons. Sudden road closures that don't match known construction activity may point to security incidents.

Data source: TomTom Traffic API — requires an API key.
Draw observation areas on the map. Traffic incidents will be fetched in real time from TomTom.
