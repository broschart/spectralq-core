Telegram Keyword-Monitor

Monitors keyword mentions across Telegram channels and groups over a configurable time period (up to 90 days).

How to use: Create keyword groups with one or more search terms. Optionally restrict the search to specific channels, or leave empty for a global search across all joined channels. The channel management section below lets you join, leave and browse available channels.

Features: Trend visualisation shows mention volume over time with daily averages and peak detection. Geographic mapping plots message origins where available. The content evolution heatmap and term shift analysis reveal how narratives develop.

Forensic relevance: Telegram is a primary platform for coordination in conflict zones, protest movements and disinformation campaigns. Tracking keyword frequency across channels helps detect mobilisation, narrative shifts and coordinated messaging. Useful for early warning of escalation.

Data source: Telegram via Telethon — requires an active Telegram session.
