Satellite Observation

Fetches current satellite imagery (Sentinel-2) for the defined area and displays it as a true-colour image.

How to use: Draw a zone on the map (max. 2° x 2°) and click "Image". The system loads the latest cloud-free Sentinel-2 image and shows the acquisition date and time. You can zoom, download the image or toggle between the satellite layer and the base map.

Forensic relevance: Satellite imagery provides visual evidence of ground-level changes — construction activity, troop movements, damage assessment, environmental changes or border modifications. Comparing images from different dates helps document the progression of events.

Data source: Copernicus Sentinel-2 (True-Color / NDVI) — requires Copernicus credentials.
Draw observation areas on the map. Sentinel-2 acquisition dates are automatically marked in the analysis timeline (under "Additional Sources").
