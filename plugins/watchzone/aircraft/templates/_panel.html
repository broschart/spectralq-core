Aircraft Tracking

Tracks aircraft in real time via ADS-B transponder data within a defined airspace.

How to use: Draw a zone on the map and click "Live". All aircraft in the area are listed with callsign, altitude, heading and speed. The map shows positions with colour-coded proximity warnings when aircraft are critically close.

Advanced features: Hourly projection calculates future positions. The heatmap visualises traffic density. Parallel coordinates allow multidimensional filtering by altitude, speed, heading and distance. Formation flight patterns and collision risks are automatically detected.

Forensic relevance: Useful for monitoring military airspace activity, detecting surveillance aircraft patterns, verifying no-fly zone compliance or reconstructing aerial incidents.

Data source: ADS-B Exchange / airplanes.live — no API key required.
